# Manual test — M5.5 (layout indicator, double right Shift, app icon)

Build: `cargo build --release -p retype`, run `target\release\retype.exe`.
Log: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\retype\retype.log`. Append results to
`docs/manual-test-log.md`.

## Tray indicator

| #  | Do                                                       | Expected                                                   |
|----|----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Start retype with EN active                              | Tray shows `EN` on blue; tooltip "retype — EN"             |
| 2  | Switch to RU with Alt+Shift / Win+Space                  | Within ~0.2 s: `RU` on coral                               |
| 3  | Convert a word (layout switches to RU)                   | Indicator follows                                          |
| 4  | Click into a window that uses another layout             | Indicator shows that window's layout                       |
| 5  | Pause (`Ctrl+Pause` or tray)                             | Grey square; tooltip "retype — paused (EN)"                |
| 6  | Install a third layout not in `layouts` (e.g. DE), use it| `DE` on dark grey                                          |
| 7  | Display scaling 150% (or 125%), sign out/in              | Letters sharp, not blurry; square fills the slot           |
| 8  | Restart Explorer                                         | Indicator comes back                                       |
| 9  | Light and dark taskbar                                   | Readable on both                                           |

## Double right Shift

| #  | Do                                                       | Expected                                                   |
|----|----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 10 | Type `ghbdtn`, tap right Shift twice quickly             | `привет`                                                   |
| 11 | Tap right Shift twice again                              | `ghbdtn` (toggle)                                          |
| 12 | Tap left Shift twice                                     | Nothing                                                    |
| 13 | Type capitals with right Shift (`Hello World`) normally  | No conversion triggered                                    |
| 14 | Tap right Shift, wait 1 s, tap again                     | Nothing                                                    |
| 15 | Hold right Shift ~1 s, release, tap once                 | Nothing                                                    |
| 16 | `double_right_shift = false`, save                       | Double tap does nothing                                    |
| 17 | In an excluded app, or paused                            | Double tap does nothing                                    |
